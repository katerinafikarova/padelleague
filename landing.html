How it works

Three steps, every week.

Create an event

Pick Americano, Mexicano, Round Robin or Playoff. Set the date, courts, spots and price. About a minute.

Send the link

Drop it in your WhatsApp group. Players see who's going, sign up and pay. No app, no account.

Play and repeat

Rounds and courts are ready. After the night you see the money and results, and copy the event to next week.

What does it cost?

Free to start. You pay a small fee per card payment. Stripe's card fee is separate. See pricing.

How do I get the money?

Players pay by card when they sign up. Stripe sends it to your bank account every week.

What if a player wants to pay cash?

Mark them as "On site" in your list. You'll still see who still owes you.
